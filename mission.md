**MISSION:** At CERAS, we’re building a humanoid robot from scratch to collaborate alongside people. Our mission is to build trust through shared learning and creative collaboration.

**GOAL:** By the end of Spring quarter, we’ll have built an upper-body humanoid robot with two articulated 7-DOF arms and a vision system to perceive its surroundings. In the coming years, we plan to add legs, integrate the full body, and develop learning policies that help the robot learn from and collaborate with people.

**PROGRESS:** With support from numerous Northwestern labs and robotics faculty, we’ve made substantial progress in mechanical design, controls, and robot learning. Explore the site to see our progress and plans.

**DEMO:** A robot drummer playing alongside and interacting with human musicians
